Information Security & Safeguards

Comprehensive standard for managing risk, security policies, asset protection, and continuous ISMS audits.

Cybersecurity Framework
Six core functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover across modern hybrid environments.
NIST SP 800-53
Federal Security & Privacy Controls
Detailed control catalog for federal agencies, defense contractors, and high-assurance enterprise systems.
CIS Controls
Critical Security Controls (v8)
Prioritized, prescriptive set of 18 safeguards designed to stop the most common cyber attack vectors.

Artificial Intelligence Trust & Safety

AI Management Systems (AIMS)
The international standard for responsible AI deployment — covering AI risk assessments, training data governance, algorithmic transparency, traceability, and human-in-the-loop oversight.

Service Organization Controls

Trust Services Criteria (AICPA)
Rigorous verification across Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy for B2B SaaS and service vendors.

Internal Controls Over Financial Reporting
SSAE 18 / ISAE 3402 reports for service organizations whose systems impact their clients' financial reporting.

Cardholder Data Security

PCI DSS (v4.0)

End-to-end payment data protection, cardholder data environment (CDE) isolation, tokenization, and QSA audit preparation.

Global Privacy & Sensitive Data

EU General Data Protection Regulation
Lawful data processing, user rights, data protection impact assessments (DPIAs), and cross-border transfer safeguards.

Consumer consent workflows, sensitive data opt-outs, and statutory security requirements.

Security, Privacy, and Breach Notification rules for protected health information (ePHI) in healthcare platforms.
